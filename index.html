02 · Private

ClientFlow

Clients, the day’s schedule, requests, and payments. One app for a small service business.

  • Home shows today’s appointments and what’s still unpaid.
  • Requests move from New to Booked to Completed.
  • Stripe marks an invoice sent. FastAPI checks that the API is up.
  1. Home Today’s brief
  2. Schedule Finish or cancel a visit
  3. Clients Search and notes
  4. Services Time and price
  5. Requests The pipeline
  6. Payments Paid, sent, overdue
  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Tailwind
  • FastAPI

03 · Public

Travlr Getaways

A beach-resort site. Home, rooms, meals, and a travel catalog of dive sites.

  • The customer pages are Handlebars, served by Express.
  • Travel lists each dive site with a photo and a short writeup.
  • MongoDB stores the trips. The Angular admin is behind a login.
  1. Home The sound of the sea
  2. Travel Dive sites
  3. Rooms Places to stay
  4. Meals Dining
  5. News Latest posts
  6. About Contact
  • Node
  • Express
  • Handlebars
  • MongoDB
  • Angular
  • JWT
